Authentic Nigerian Whot Rules

Objective

Be the first player to play all cards in your hand. When you drop your final card, shout "Check Up!" to claim victory.

The 54-Card Authentic Deck

Divided into 5 shapes: Circle (12), Triangle (12), Cross (9), Square (9), Star (8), plus 4 special Whot 20 wildcards.

Special Action Cards

1 Hold On: The player plays again immediately.
2 Pick Two: Next player draws 2 cards & loses turn.
5 Pick Three: Next player draws 3 cards & loses turn.
8 Suspension: The next player is skipped.
14 General Market: EVERY opponent draws 1 card!
20 Whot Wildcard: Can be played on anything to demand a shape.

Turn Timer & Disqualification

Players have 25 seconds per turn. Missing a turn yields a +1 penalty card and a Penalty Strike. A player on strike receives 35 seconds next turn; failing again leads to instant disqualification.

Last Card Call

When you have only 1 card remaining, you must alert the table with "Last Card!". Our automated whistle alerts all players.
